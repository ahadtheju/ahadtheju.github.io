shows a gold placeholder with the filename on it, so nothing looks broken.

The fastest way: run `bash download-images.sh` from the folder above this one.
It pulls all 26 from the old Google Site. If that fails, save them by hand from
the old site using the table below.

## Worth checking

I matched photo to caption by the order they appear in the old site's HTML.
That is a guess, not something the old site labelled. The six attire photos and
the three Stay photos are the ones most likely to be shuffled, since their
captions are interchangeable. Open `faqs.html` and `stay.html` after
downloading and swap any filenames that look wrong.

## Sizes

Cards crop to 4:3, attire photos to 3:4 (portrait), banners to 16:7. The crop is
centred, so a face near the edge of a photo can get clipped. If that happens,
crop the JPG yourself before dropping it in. Anything around 1200px on the long
edge is plenty.
